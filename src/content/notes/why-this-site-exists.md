I wanted a space that feels simple, personal, and useful. Not just a list of projects, but a place to collect work, notes, and the ideas I keep returning to.

## What I wanted

I wanted this site to feel calm, clear, and personal. Not overly polished in a corporate way, but thoughtful enough to reflect how I think about software and the things I care about.

## Why notes matter here

The notes section is important because a lot of what I’m interested in doesn’t fit neatly into project cards. Some things are better captured as reflections, technical notes, and ideas still taking shape.
