I’m especially interested in tools that make systems easier to inspect, understand, and work with. I like software that is calm, practical, and clear about what it is doing.

## What draws me in

I’m drawn to tools that reduce confusion rather than add more abstraction. I like software that gives visibility into what is happening and makes it easier to reason about systems.

## What I keep coming back to

Again and again, I find myself interested in tools around observability, diagnostics, local-first software, privacy, and interfaces that help people understand complexity without flattening it.
